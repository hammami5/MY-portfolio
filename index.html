Featured · Full-Stack Web App P.01

BioStore

A complete e-commerce experience for organic and bio products — catalog, cart, checkout and admin panel, designed and built from scratch as a real product.

The Problem

Small bio-products sellers are scattered across social media. There's no single place to browse a catalog, compare and order — and no easy way for the seller to manage products.

The Idea

One clean storefront where products live in a real database, customers browse and order, and the owner manages everything from a simple admin panel.

The Solution

A full-stack application: PHP and MySQL power the data layer, with a responsive front-end for the shop and a protected admin area for managing products, orders and inventory.
